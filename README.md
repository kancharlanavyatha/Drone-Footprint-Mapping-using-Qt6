# Drone Footprint Mapping & Visualizer (Qt6)

An interactive, high-performance GIS desktop application that extracts drone telemetry, calculates ground footprints following an industry-standard 10-step mapping workflow, and projects them onto a 3D point cloud terrain. 

The project includes a modular **Python Preprocessing Pipeline**, an interactive **C++ Qt6 Visualizer** (linked with GDAL), and a matching **Python PySide6 (Qt6) Visualizer**.

## 📐 The 10-Step GIS Mapping Workflow

### Phase A: Collection & Extraction
*   **Step 1: Collect Drone Images** $\rightarrow$ Raw photos stored in the `images/` directory.
*   **Step 2: Extract GPS Position** $\rightarrow$ Handled in **`step1_extract_metadata.py`** (Extracts `GPSLatitude`, `GPSLongitude`, `GPSAltitude` decimal numbers).
*   **Step 3: Extract Camera Orientation** $\rightarrow$ Handled in **`step1_extract_metadata.py`** (Extracts `GimbalYawDegree`, `GimbalPitchDegree`, `GimbalRollDegree`).
*   **Step 4: Extract Calibration Info** $\rightarrow$ Handled in **`step1_extract_metadata.py`** (Extracts `FocalLength`, `ImageWidth`, `ImageHeight`).

### Phase B: Geometry & Field of View (FOV)
*   **Step 5: Determine Sensor Size** $\rightarrow$ Defined in **`step2_sensor_and_fov.py`** (DJI Phantom 3 utilizes a $6.17\text{ mm} \times 4.55\text{ mm}$ physical sensor).
*   **Step 6: Calculate Camera FOV** $\rightarrow$ Computed in **`step2_sensor_and_fov.py`**:
    $$\text{FOV} = 2 \cdot \arctan\left(\frac{\text{Sensor Size}}{2 \cdot \text{Focal Length}}\right)$$
    *Calculates $81.03^\circ$ horizontal and $64.44^\circ$ vertical viewing angles.*

### Phase C: Projection & Bounding Box
*   **Step 7: Compute Ground Coverage** $\rightarrow$ Calculated in **`step3_ground_coverage.py`**:
    $$\text{Ground Width} = 2 \cdot \text{Altitude} \cdot \tan\left(\frac{\text{FOV}_h}{2}\right) \approx 1.71 \cdot \text{Altitude}$$
    $$\text{Ground Height} = 2 \cdot \text{Altitude} \cdot \tan\left(\frac{\text{FOV}_v}{2}\right) \approx 1.28 \cdot \text{Altitude}$$
*   **Step 8: Compute 4 Ground Corners** $\rightarrow$ Calculated in **`step3_ground_coverage.py`** by converting GPS coordinates to local UTM Zone 15N coordinates (meters) and applying a 2D rotation matrix based on gimbal yaw orientation:
    $$X_{\text{rotated}} = X_{\text{local}} \cdot \cos(\text{yaw}) + Y_{\text{local}} \cdot \sin(\text{yaw})$$
    $$Y_{\text{rotated}} = -X_{\text{local}} \cdot \sin(\text{yaw}) + Y_{\text{local}} \cdot \cos(\text{yaw})$$

### Phase D: Export
*   **Step 9: Build the Polygon** $\rightarrow$ Structured in **`step4_polygon_export.py`** by closing the coordinates loop (joining corner 4 back to corner 1).
*   **Step 10: Export to GIS Format** $\rightarrow$ Handled in **`step4_polygon_export.py`** by transforming coordinates back to GPS standard (WGS 84 degrees) and exporting to `footprints_pipeline.geojson`.

## 💻 Visualizer UI Features

1.  **3D Map Canvas**: Draws the LiDAR beach point cloud color-coded by elevation (Z height). Renders the flight path, interactive photo node markers, and ground footprints.
2.  **Dynamic Metadata Table**: Select any photo node or list item to display a complete list of **all EXIF tags** (calibration, orientation, GPS coordinates) in a copyable table view (`QTableWidget`).
3.  **Orthophoto Warped Overlay**: Dynamically rotates, scales, and warps the drone image onto its exact ground footprint directly over the point cloud.
4.  **GDAL Elevation Query**: Hovering the mouse over the map canvas queries the underlying Digital Surface Model (`dsm.tif`) using GDAL in real-time, showing the exact terrain elevation (meters) in the status bar.
5.  **Scrollable Sidebar Panel**: Sidebar elements adapt dynamically to any screen height and window scale via `QScrollArea`.

*   **Python 3.10+** (with packages `pyproj`)
*   **ExifTool** (added to system PATH)
*   **Qt6** and **GDAL** C++ library binaries

& "C:\Users\knrao\AppData\Local\Programs\Python\Python310\python.exe" "d:\DRDO\drone points\python_qt6_visualizer\main.py"

### 3. Running the C++ Visualizer (Qt Creator)
1. Open **Qt Creator**, click **Open Project**, and select:
   `d:\DRDO\drone points\cpp_qt6_visualizer\CMakeLists.txt`
2. Go to the **Projects** tab (left sidebar) $\rightarrow$ select **Run Settings** (next to Build Settings).
3. Under the **Environment** section, click **Details** next to *Use Build Environment*.
4. Select the **`PATH`** variable, click **Edit**, and prepend `C:\msys64\mingw64\bin;` to the very beginning.
5. Press the green **Play** button (or `Ctrl + R`) to compile and launch!
